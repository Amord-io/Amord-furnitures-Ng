About AmorD-Ng Furnitures

At AmorD-Ng Furnitures, we are dedicated to transforming your living spaces into havens of comfort, elegance, and functionality. As a premier furniture store in Nigeria, we specialize in crafting and delivering high-quality sofas, dining tables, kitchen units, TV stands, and bed frames that perfectly blend modern design with enduring durability. Our mission is simple: to bring world-class furniture within reach of every Nigerian home. We understand that furniture is an investment. That's why we focus on premium materials, meticulous craftsmanship, and customer-centric service.

Payment & Delivery Policy

🚚 Free Delivery: We offer free delivery on all orders within major cities in Nigeria.

💳 Flexible Payment Options: We offer two convenient payment methods:
1. Pay Half, Pay Balance on Delivery: Pay 50% upfront, settle the remaining 50% when delivered.
2. Pay Fully Upfront: Pay 100% now and enjoy priority delivery.

Privacy Policy

At AmorD-Ng Furnitures, your privacy is paramount. We only collect the information necessary to process your orders. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your data.
