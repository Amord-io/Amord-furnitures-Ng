About AmorD-Ng Furnitures

At AmorD-Ng Furnitures, we are dedicated to transforming your living spaces into havens of comfort, elegance, and functionality. As a premier furniture store in Nigeria, we specialize in crafting and delivering high-quality sofas, dining tables, kitchen units, TV stands, and bed frames that perfectly blend modern design with enduring durability.

Our mission is simple: to bring world-class furniture within reach of every Nigerian home. Whether you are furnishing a new apartment, upgrading your living room, or searching for that perfect statement piece, our diverse collection ensures you find exactly what you need.

We understand that furniture is an investment. That's why we focus on premium materials, meticulous craftsmanship, and customer-centric service. With AmorD-Ng, you're not just buying furniture; you're investing in quality, comfort, and a home that truly reflects your style.

Payment & Delivery Policy

🚚 Free Delivery: We offer free delivery on all orders within major cities in Nigeria.

💳 Flexible Payment Options: We understand that flexibility is key. We offer two convenient payment methods:

  • Pay Half, Pay Balance on Delivery: Reserve your furniture by paying 50% upfront, and settle the remaining 50% when your furniture is delivered to your doorstep.
  • Pay Fully Upfront: For a seamless checkout, you can choose to pay 100% of the price now and enjoy priority delivery.

Our goal is to make your furniture buying experience smooth, secure, and stress-free.
